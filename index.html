Box থেকে Phrase বসানো (Q.2) — Last 10 years-এ সবচেয়ে বেশি আসা ১৫টি phrase: প্রতি বছর box-এ ১০টি থাকে। নিচের সবগুলো ভালো করে শিখলে সব সেটে common পাবে।
Phrase ১ — as if (যেন, মনে হচ্ছে)
★★★ প্রায় প্রতি বছর আসে
নিয়ম: বাস্তবে যা সত্য নয় কিন্তু মনে হচ্ছে এমন ভাব বোঝাতে।
Structure: Subject + verb + as if + Subject + were/had + ...
  • He acts as if he were a king. (সে আচরণ করে যেন সে রাজা — কিন্তু সে রাজা নয়)
  • She talks as if she had been a doctor. (সে কথা বলে যেন সে ডাক্তার ছিল)
  • My friend talks as if he were a millionaire. (বাস্তবে সে গরিব)
⚠️ চেনার উপায়: বাক্যে "কিন্তু সত্যিটা ভিন্ন" এই ইঙ্গিত থাকলে → as if
Phrase ২ — lest (পাছে, যাতে না)
★★★ সবচেয়ে বেশি আসে Clause/Phrase section-এ
নিয়ম: ভয় বা আশঙ্কা থেকে সতর্কতা বোঝাতে।
Structure: ... lest + Subject + should + verb (base)
  • Walk fast lest you should miss the bus.
  • Study hard lest you should fail.
  • He ran fast lest he should be late.
⚠️ চেনার উপায়: "পাছে না/যাতে না" — সবসময় lest + should
Phrase ৩ — unless (যদি না)
★★★ প্রতি বছর আসে
নিয়ম: "যদি না" শর্ত বোঝাতে। Unless মানেই "if not"।
Structure: Unless + Subject + verb (present), Subject + will/can + verb
  • Unless you work hard, you will not succeed.
  • Unless you are honest, nobody will trust you.
  • You cannot prosper in life unless you use your time properly.
⚠️ মনে রেখো: Unless-এর পরে NOT ব্যবহার হয় না (কারণ unless = if not)
Phrase ৪ — it is high time (এখনই সময়)
★★★ প্রতি বছর আসে
নিয়ম: "এখনই উপযুক্ত সময়" বোঝাতে।
Structure: It is high time + Subject + verb (past tense) / to + verb
  • It is high time we took steps against corruption. (past tense)
  • It is high time we started learning English.
  • It is high time we practiced patriotism.
⚠️ "high time"-এর পরে verb সবসময় past tense হবে!
Phrase ৫ — no sooner had...than (যেই...অমনি)
★★★ প্রতি বছর আসে
নিয়ম: দুটো কাজ একসাথে/সাথে সাথে হওয়া বোঝাতে। Inverted structure ব্যবহার হয়।
Structure: No sooner had + Sub + V3 + than + Sub + V2
  • No sooner had the teacher entered than the students kept quiet.
  • No sooner had I heard the news than I ran there.
  • No sooner had he got the loaf than the crow flew away.
⚠️ সবসময় "No sooner had... than" — কখনো "as soon as" দিয়ে বদলাবে না এই structure-এ
Phrase ৬ — would you mind (আপনি কি করবেন?)
★★☆ প্রায়ই আসে
নিয়ম: ভদ্রভাবে কারো সাহায্য চাইতে।
Structure: Would you mind + verb+ing...
  • Would you mind lending me your phone? (lend নয়, lending)
  • Would you mind opening the window?
  • Would you mind waiting here for a moment?
⚠️ Would you mind-এর পরে সবসময় verb+ing (gerund)!
Phrase ৭ — what if (যদি...তাহলে কী হবে?)
★★☆ প্রায়ই আসে
নিয়ম: অনিশ্চিত পরিস্থিতি বা ভয়ের প্রশ্ন করতে।
Structure: What if + Subject + verb...
  • What if we miss the bus? (যদি বাস মিস হয়ে যায়?)
  • What if he wouldn't have stopped the bus?
  • Rina: What if we miss the train? Mina: We'll take a taxi.
⚠️ চেনার উপায়: প্রশ্ন করছে, পরিণাম নিয়ে দুশ্চিন্তা আছে
Phrase ৮ — the + comparative...the + comparative
★★☆ মাঝে মাঝে আসে
নিয়ম: একটা বাড়লে আরেকটাও বাড়ে বোঝাতে।
Structure: The + comparative, the + comparative
  • The older you grow, the more experienced you become.
  • The more you read, the more you learn.
  • The harder you work, the better result you get.
Phrase ৯ — I wish (আমি যদি...হতাম)
★★☆ প্রায়ই আসে
নিয়ম: অসম্ভব বা অপ্রাপ্ত ইচ্ছা বোঝাতে।
Structure: I wish + Subject + were/had/could + verb
  • I wish I were a bird. (am নয়, were)
  • I wish I could help the poor.
  • I wish I had wings of a bird.
⚠️ I wish-এর পরে সবসময় Past Tense বা Past Perfect!
Phrase ১০ — so that / in order that (যাতে)
★★☆
নিয়ম: উদ্দেশ্য বোঝাতে।
Structure: ... so that + Subject + can/may/could/might + verb
  • I study hard so that I can pass the exam.
  • He took a job so that he could earn some money.
  • She came to my room in order that she could learn something.
Box Phrase মনে রাখার কৌশল: as if = বাস্তবে মিথ্যা | lest = পাছে না (+ should) | unless = যদি না | it is high time = এখনই সময় (+ past) | no sooner had...than = সাথে সাথে | would you mind = ভদ্রভাবে অনুরোধ (+ ing) | I wish = অসম্ভব ইচ্ছা (+ were/could)
Clause/Phrase দিয়ে Completing Sentence (Q.3/4) — Last 10 years-এ সবচেয়ে বেশি আসা Structures: নিচের প্রতিটি structure কমপক্ষে ৩-৫ বার বিভিন্ন board-এ এসেছে।
Structure ১ — If Clause (Conditional)
★★★ প্রতি বছর আসে — ৩ ধরনের If Clause
ধরনStructureউদাহরণ
Type 1 — সম্ভব শর্তIf + Present, will + V1If you work hard, you will succeed.
Type 2 — অবাস্তব বর্তমানIf + Past, would + V1If I had money, I would help the poor.
Type 3 — অতীতের অবাস্তবIf + Past Perfect, would have + V3If I had worked hard, I would have passed.
  • If you maintain honesty, you will be respected.
  • If I had a camera, I would take some photographs.
  • If I had earned enough money, I would have helped the poor.
Structure ২ — Though/Although (যদিও)
★★★ প্রতি বছর আসে
Structure: Though/Although + clause, main clause (বিপরীত ফলাফল)
  • Though Bangladesh is a small country, it has many heritage sites.
  • Though he was brilliant, he did not score well.
  • Though I am poor, I hate begging.
⚠️ Though-এর দুই অংশে বিপরীত ধারণা থাকবে। "but" ব্যবহার করা যাবে না।
Structure ৩ — so...that / too...to (এতটাই যে / এত...যে পারে না)
★★★
Structureউদাহরণ
so + adj/adv + that + clauseHe is so tired that he cannot walk.
too + adj + to + verbHe is too weak to walk. (too...to = negative meaning)
adj + enough + to + verbHe is strong enough to lift the box.
Structure ৪ — with a view to / by / in order to (উদ্দেশ্যে)
★★☆
Structure: with a view to + verb+ing (gerund) / in order to + verb (infinitive)
  • He came here with a view to helping me. (helping — gerund)
  • We work hard with a view to building the nation.
  • She studies in order to pass the exam. (pass — infinitive)
⚠️ "with a view to"-এর পরে সবসময় verb+ing! "to pass" নয়, "to passing"!
Structure ৫ — It is/was + time/long since (এটা অনেক দিন হলো)
★★☆
  • It is a long time since we met last. (since-এর পরে Past Tense)
  • It was five years since we last saw each other.
  • It is long since I visited my village.
Structure ৬ — Had I / Were I (Inverted Conditional)
★★☆ HSC 2023, 2024-এ এসেছে
Structure: Had + Subject + V3, Subject + would have + V3
  • Had I earned enough money, I would have helped the poor.
  • Had he studied seriously, he would have got GPA 5.
  • Had we reached earlier, we would not have missed the train.
Structure ৭ — lest (পাছে না) — Clause-এ ব্যবহার
★★★ প্রতি বছর আসে
  • Walk fast lest you should miss the bus.
  • Study regularly lest you should fail the examination.
  • Lock the door lest a thief should enter.
Structure ৮ — Whenever / As soon as (যখনই)
★★☆
  • Whenever he speaks in English, he makes mistakes.
  • As soon as the teacher entered, the students kept quiet.
  • As soon as I saw him, I informed the police.
Clause Completing-এর মূল কৌশল: (১) আগের অংশের Tense দেখো → পরের অংশ কী Tense হবে বোঝা যাবে। (২) Though/Although দেখলে → বিপরীত কিছু লেখো। (৩) Lest দেখলে → should + base verb। (৪) Had + V3 দেখলে → would have + V3। (৫) with a view to দেখলে → verb+ing।

Box থেকে Phrase বসানো — ৫টি Practice Set
Common Box (সব set-এ): as if  |  lest  |  unless  |  it is high time  |  no sooner had...than  |  would you mind  |  what if  |  I wish  |  so that  |  the more...the more
Box Set 1
(a) He is very lazy. He always acts ___ he were busy.
(b) Keep the medicine carefully ___ children should eat it by mistake.
(c) You will never get a good job ___ you learn English properly.
(d) Corruption is destroying the country. ___ we stopped it.
(e) ___ the teacher entered the class ___ the students became silent.
(a) as if  |  (b) lest  |  (c) unless  |  (d) It is high time  |  (e) No sooner had...than

Clause/Phrase দিয়ে Completing — ৫টি Practice Set
Clause Set 1 (Dhaka Board 2024 style)
(a) Trees are essential for our existence. So, we should ___
(b) Though Bangladesh is a small country, ___
(c) The exam will start soon. Walk fast lest ___
(d) He came to me with a view to ___
(e) If you had worked hard in school, ___
(a) plant more trees and protect the environment
(b) it has many natural resources and heritage sites
(c) you should miss it / you should be late
(d) seeking my help / asking for advice
(e) you would have got a good result
Clause Set 2 (Cumilla Board 2024 style)
(a) Corruption is a great problem. It is high time ___
(b) English is important. I wish ___
(c) He is walking slowly lest ___
(d) Time once lost never returns. Unless you use time properly, ___
(e) Had I known his address, ___
(a) we took effective steps against corruption
(b) I could speak English fluently
(c) he should fall on the road
(d) you cannot prosper in life
(e) I would have contacted him
Clause Set 3 (Mymensingh Board 2024 style)
(a) Patriotism is a noble virtue. It is high time ___
(b) Hasan got GPA 4.50. Had he studied seriously, ___
(c) Those who waste time ___
(d) Ayesha studies hard so that ___
(e) The man walked very slowly lest ___
(a) we practiced patriotism in real life
(b) he would have got GPA 5.00
(c) suffer in the long run / cannot succeed
(d) she can secure GPA-5 in the exam
(e) he should fall down on the road
Clause Set 4 (Rajshahi Board style)
(a) I cannot recall his face. It is a long time since ___
(b) She came to the hospital with a view to ___
(c) Though he tried very hard, ___
(d) The box is very heavy. He is strong enough ___
(e) He was so angry that ___
(a) we last met / I saw him
(b) seeing a doctor / getting treatment
(c) he could not succeed in the exam
(d) to lift it / to carry it alone
(e) he could not speak a word
Clause Set 5 (All Board Mixed style)
(a) We are a free nation now. 1971 is the year when ___
(b) Whenever my uncle visits us, ___
(c) No sooner had I heard the accident news ___
(d) I was too tired ___
(e) Unless we plant more trees, ___
(a) we achieved our independence
(b) he brings gifts for everyone
(c) than I rushed to the hospital
(d) to continue working / to walk any further
(e) the environment will be destroyed / we will suffer from natural disasters
